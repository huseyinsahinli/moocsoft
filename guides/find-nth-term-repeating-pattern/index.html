How to Find the 100th Term of a Repeating Pattern

Write one complete cycle, subtract one from the position you want, then divide by the cycle length. The remainder counts how many steps to move from the first symbol. In red, blue, blue on repeat, the 100th term is red.

The useful takeaway

Position 1 starts at offset 0. Using (position − 1) before taking the remainder handles exact multiples and first positions with the same rule.

Read the boundary between two cycles

Make a bracelet by repeating red, blue, blue. Positions 1, 2 and 3 finish the first cycle. The two blue beads occupy different slots even though they look the same. Choosing a slot by counting distinct colors would give a cycle of two and the wrong answer.

Check whether a printed block can shrink

For the block A B A B, two copies of A B fill all four slots exactly. Using a period of two gives the same terms with smaller numbers. A B A is different: when that whole block loops, its boundary is A followed by A. Shortening it to A B would erase that boundary.

The repeating pattern calculator checks which smaller blocks tile your input exactly. Supply a block that the puzzle actually says repeats. A few observed terms alone cannot determine the continuation.

Try three positions before opening the answers

1. Bell, drum, clap, rest repeats. What is term 64?

Rest. The first 63 terms contain 15 full cycles plus three beats. Term 64 is the fourth beat.

2. The printed block is 2 2 7 2 2 7. What is term 31?

2. The shortest unit has three slots. The first 30 terms fill ten cycles, so term 31 returns to slot one.

3. A B A repeats. What is term 5?

B. Write two copies: A B A | A B A. Slot two of the second copy is position five.

Common questions

What does a remainder of zero mean?

Using (position − 1) modulo period, zero selects the first term. If you divide the original position instead, zero selects the last term.

Can the calculator predict an unknown sequence?

It assumes the complete block you supply repeats indefinitely. Several different rules can fit a short sample.
