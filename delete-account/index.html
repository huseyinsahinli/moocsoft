Math Riddles accounts are anonymous — we never ask for your name, email or phone number. Your account exists only so that battle mode can track your trophies, and you can remove it at any time.

Fastest — takes a few seconds

Delete it from inside the app

This removes everything immediately, with no waiting for us.

  1. Open Math Riddles.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner of the main menu.
  3. Scroll to Delete my data at the bottom of Settings.
  4. Tap it, then confirm. Your account and data are erased right away.
Alternative — up to 30 days

Or submit a request here

Use this if you have already uninstalled the app. We need your friend code to find the right account — it is the only thing that identifies an anonymous account.

Your friend code is shown in the app under Settings → Your code, and looks like K7M2QX. If you no longer have it, email us and we will help.

Only used to confirm the deletion, then discarded.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous account
  • Your nickname and friend code
  • Your trophies, energy, wins and losses
  • Your match history

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you. Aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as total matches played) may remain, because they can no longer be linked back to any person. Single-player puzzle progress is stored only on your own device, so uninstalling the app removes it.

How long it takes

Deleting from inside the app is immediate. Requests submitted through this form are actioned manually, normally within a few days and always within 30 days.
