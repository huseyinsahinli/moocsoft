The workout flow

Nothing between you and the next set.

01

SET-BY-SET TARGETS

Give every set its own reps and kg target, or use seconds for a timed movement.

02

AUTOMATIC REST

Completing a set starts the countdown. Pause it, adjust it, skip it or end it immediately.

03

REAL EQUIPMENT

Machine, cable, dumbbell, barbell, bench, bodyweight or your own setup.

04

RESUMES LOCALLY

Your active exercise, current set and rest time survive closing the app. No account is required.

Using Did You Lift

Make the first step practical.

Keep targets and actual results distinct

Plan each set, then record the reps and kilograms you completed. Use seconds for timed movements and exercise notes for setup details. A consistent record makes the next comparable session easier to understand.

Make the next session easier to follow

Organize your chosen program across the week, check equipment and rest settings, then follow the active set on screen. The guides below explain logging conventions and volume calculations; they do not prescribe a training program. Optional lifetime Premium adds planning and sharing features.
