04 / CHECK YOUR SETUP

Match the layout before changing buttons.

  1. Open the in-game settings.Find Control Scheme, as shown in the supplied image.
  2. Read the selected preset.This reference uses Default. The screenshot shows a preset selector, not evidence that every preset uses the same bindings.
  3. Choose the right preview.Compare On Foot while walking. Use the corresponding vehicle context when driving.

For a download or launch problem, use PlayStation’s GTA VI support page. Console system settings are separate from the game’s Control Scheme menu.

PlayStation lists the PS5 release as November 19, 2026, at midnight local time. That means availability can differ by region while it is still November 18 elsewhere.

Where does GTA 6 take place?

Vice City is a city within the state of Leonida. When a character talks about the Keys or the Grassrivers, they’re talking about other parts of that same state. Rockstar’s place profiles are a good way to get your bearings before following Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos through the story.

Vice City
Look for dense streets, beachfront hotels and nightlife. Start here if a screenshot shows a crowded urban coastline.
Leonida Keys
A chain of islands with marinas and waterfront communities. Jason’s background connects him to the Keys.
Grassrivers
Wetlands and waterways. These are the landscapes to look for if you want to explore Leonida beyond its urban streets.

Why hasn’t my PS5 download started?

  1. Check the queue.Open Downloads/Uploads from the control center. If GTA VI is listed but stalled, pause the download and resume it.
  2. Check available space.Open Settings → Storage. Compare the available space with the size shown for your download.
  3. Check the account.If the game is missing from your library, sign in with the account that bought it or redeemed the code and check its order history.

05 / QUICK QUESTIONS

GTA 6 controls FAQ

How do I open the weapon selection menu in GTA 6 on PS5?

Hold L1 to open Loadout, according to the linked PS5 controls report. A normal L1 press is listed as Holster. Check your selected control scheme if the result differs.

How do I get into a car in GTA 6?

Triangle is the interaction button in the supplied Default / On Foot screenshot. Approach the vehicle and follow its entry prompt. The linked vehicle controls report confirms Triangle for exiting; it does not separately document entering, so entry is not independently confirmed here.

How do I open the map and pause menu?

The supplied PS5 settings screenshot shows the touchpad for Map and the Options button for Pause. These are different controls.

Are these Xbox Series X|S controls too?

No. This page covers PS5 only. An Xbox controller layout has not been independently checked for this guide.

Why does a button do something different in my game?

First compare the active control scheme and the current context: on foot, aiming or driving. This guide is based on a Default / On Foot screenshot and a separate published PS5 controls report, not a hands-on test by Moocsoft.
