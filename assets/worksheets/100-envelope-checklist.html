100 envelope challenge checklist

Complete each number once, in any order. In the classic version, envelope 37 means a $37 contribution. Tick the box after you have set aside the money. One contribution per day is optional: 100 days is not a required deadline.

Classic $1–$100: $5,050 total · Half-size $0.50–$50: $2,525 · Tenth-size $0.10–$10: $505
