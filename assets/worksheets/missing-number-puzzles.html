Missing-number answers and checks

Compare your method with the stated rule, then substitute your answer back into every row.

1. Complete a balanced equation Answer: 29

Subtract 18 from both sides: 47 − 18 = 29. Substitute 29 into the original equation to check your answer.

  • 29 + 18 = 47.

2. Find the repeated row total Answer: 19

The first two numbers add to the result in every row. Apply the same rule to the final row: 7 + 12 = 19.

  • 6 + 9 = 15.
  • 8 + 5 = 13.
  • 7 + 12 = 19.

3. Multiply, then make one adjustment Answer: 22

Each result is the product of the first two numbers minus 2. The final row is (6 × 4) − 2 = 22. Subtract after multiplying; subtracting 2 from an input would change the grouping.

  • (3 × 4) − 2 = 10.
  • (5 × 3) − 2 = 13.
  • (6 × 4) − 2 = 22.

4. Recover the missing input Answer: 6

The final row means 4 × □ + 4 = 28. Subtract 4 to get 4 × □ = 24, then divide by 4 to get 6. Put 6 back into the original rule to check it.

  • 2 × 5 + 2 = 12.
  • 3 × 4 + 3 = 15.
  • 4 × 6 + 4 = 28.

5. Compare two squared values Answer: 56

Square both inputs separately. In the final row, 9² − 5² = 81 − 25 = 56. Squaring the difference would be a different operation: (9 − 5)² = 16.

  • 5² − 3² = 25 − 9 = 16.
  • 7² − 4² = 49 − 16 = 33.
  • 9² − 5² = 81 − 25 = 56.
