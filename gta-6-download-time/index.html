How long will my GTA 6 download take?

Use the amount left in the download queue. A console showing 80 GB of 120 GB has 40 GB left. At a steady 50 Mbps, that takes about 1 hour 47 minutes. Those figures are an example. Use the size your console reports for your edition and update.

What if the speed keeps changing?

Write down the progress twice, five minutes apart. Suppose it moves from 80 to 82 GB out of 120. That is 2 GB per five minutes, or 0.4 GB per minute. The remaining 38 GB would take about 95 minutes at that pace. This method uses the rate your console actually sustained over the sample.

Why can the console finish later?

Internet plans usually quote megabits per second. Download queues often show gigabytes. Eight bits make one byte, and the estimate uses decimal GB. Shared Wi-Fi, changing server speeds and pauses affect the transfer. Installation time comes afterward. If the queue stops moving, check PlayStation’s GTA VI download help.
