A thoughtful note

Useful guidance, not medical advice.

Nutrition values and allergen detection are AI-generated estimates and can be imperfect. Always check packaging, recipes and professional guidance when an allergy, health condition or medical decision is involved.

Questions

Before your first scan.

Do I need to log every ingredient?

No. Take a photo or choose one from your library and NutriLens analyzes the visible meal for you.

Can NutriLens scan restaurant and homemade food?

Yes. It is designed for plated meals, homemade dishes, restaurant food, snacks and leftovers — not only packaged products.

Are the calorie and macro results exact?

They are AI-powered estimates. Portion size, hidden ingredients and preparation methods can affect accuracy.
