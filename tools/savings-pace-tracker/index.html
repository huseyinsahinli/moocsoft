Weekly Savings Tracker: Plan vs. Actual Deposits

Use this free savings tracking tool to compare weekly deposits with your plan. See how far ahead or behind you are and what to set aside next week.

Try it with your numbers

Use one currency and plain numbers. Enter 0 for a completed week with no deposit and a negative amount for a withdrawal. Leave future weeks out. A blank list means the plan has just started.

Track your real deposits

Create a goal in Frugal and record the money you actually set aside.

Worked example

With 60 at the start and a target of 300, six weeks call for 40 per week. Deposits of 40, 0 and 55 leave 155 after week three. The planned balance is 180, so the gap is 25. The remaining 145 can be split into 48.34, 48.33 and 48.33.
