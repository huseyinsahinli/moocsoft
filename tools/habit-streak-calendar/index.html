Use a streak without letting it use you.

A streak makes repeated action visible, but it is not the habit itself. The useful part is noticing when, where and how often you can keep the promise.

  1. Choose one action small enough to complete on a difficult day.
  2. Mark the day only after the action is genuinely finished.
  3. If the chain breaks, restart immediately instead of waiting for a perfect Monday.

Current streak versus longest streak

The current streak counts consecutive completions ending today or yesterday. The longest streak is the best uninterrupted run across every locally saved date.
