How to finish a savings challenge.

A traditional 52-week challenge increases the deposit every week. Starting with $5 and adding $5 each week means saving $5 in week one, $10 in week two and $260 in week 52.

  1. Choose a first deposit that feels almost too easy.
  2. Pick a weekly increase you can still afford near the end.
  3. Automate the transfer or mark it complete on the same day each week.

Make the plan easier when needed

You can reverse the order and begin with the largest deposits, keep the weekly amount flat or pause without abandoning the goal. Consistency matters more than following one challenge format perfectly.

Compare the contribution schedule, not just the total

A $1 starting deposit with a $1 weekly increase totals $1,378 across 52 contributions, but the last four deposits total $202. Check those larger weeks before choosing the plan. Set the increase to zero to compare a flat contribution.

The calculator uses a nonnegative increase. For a reverse challenge, calculate the matching increasing version and follow its amounts backward. Results exclude interest, fees and withdrawals; the app records real contributions after you make them.
