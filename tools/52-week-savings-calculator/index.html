How to finish a savings challenge.

A traditional 52-week challenge increases the deposit every week. Starting with $1 and adding $1 each week means saving $1 in week one, $2 in week two and $52 in week 52.

  1. Choose an affordable first deposit.
  2. Pick a weekly increase you can still afford near the end.
  3. Make the transfer and mark it complete on the same day each week.

Make the plan easier when needed

You can follow the chart backward and begin with the largest deposits, or set the increase to zero for a flat weekly amount. The total is a plan, not money automatically saved.

This tool is for planning and education. It does not include interest, investment returns, fees, inflation or tax and is not financial advice.
