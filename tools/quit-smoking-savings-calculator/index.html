Make invisible progress visible.

The early days of quitting can feel slow. A progress calculation turns each smoke-free hour into something concrete: money kept, cigarettes avoided and minutes available for something else.

  1. Use your actual quit date or the moment your current attempt began.
  2. Enter an honest average for cigarettes and pack price.
  3. Return when you need proof that a difficult day has not erased your progress.

A reset does not erase what you learned

If you smoke again, seek support and begin from the most honest starting point. Progress is not a moral score, and professional help can make quitting safer and more effective.

This calculator tracks cost and time estimates only. It does not calculate health recovery or provide medical treatment. For quitting support, medication questions or urgent symptoms, contact a qualified healthcare professional or your local stop-smoking service.

Read the money and time estimates correctly

The calculator multiplies your previous daily smoking cost by time since the quit date. Pack price, cigarettes per pack and your baseline use all affect the estimate. The output assumes you have not smoked during that period.

Money avoided is not automatically money deposited into savings. The time result estimates smoke-break minutes, not additional lifespan. Keep a separate record of actual savings and use the journal guides for context a counter cannot show.
