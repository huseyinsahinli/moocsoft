Questions

A better first day.

What habits can I track?

Smoking, vaping, alcohol, social media, procrastination, junk food, gambling, gaming, nail biting or a custom habit of your own.

What happens if I reset a habit?

Your current timer starts again while the reset remains part of your history, helping you learn without treating progress as all-or-nothing.

Can I track more than one habit?

Yes. QuitBit supports multiple active targets so each habit can have its own timing and goal.

Make the first step practical.

See more than the current timer

Keep support in the plan

Use the trigger-journal and restart worksheets to keep an honest record of your experience. QuitBit is a tracking companion, not a treatment for nicotine dependence. A healthcare professional or quit-smoking service can help you choose appropriate cessation support.
