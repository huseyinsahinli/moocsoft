Can you play GTA 6 from a USB drive on PS5?

To play the PS5 version of GTA 6, keep the game on console storage or a compatible installed M.2 SSD. A USB extended storage drive can hold a PS5 game between sessions. To launch it again, move it back to one of the two playable locations.

Pick a destination before moving a game

Play tonight
Check free space on console storage or your installed M.2 SSD. Choose the one that can hold the installation.
Make room for GTA 6
Move a different PS5 game that you rarely play to USB extended storage. That frees space on its original drive.
Keep your progress
The installed game and its saved data have separate storage controls. Leave saved data in place when your goal is to move the installation.

Work out the space shortfall

Open Settings → Storage and read the available space for the destination. Compare it with the requirement your console shows. For example, if an installation asks for 110 GB and that drive has 84 GB free, the gap is 26 GB. Moving a 32 GB game off that drive would leave 116 GB free in this example. The numbers illustrate the calculation; your GTA 6 installation or update supplies the actual requirement.

I moved the game but the install still won't fit

Check which drive gained the free space. Moving a game off the M.2 SSD helps an installation targeting the M.2 SSD. If the target is console storage, check the console storage figure again. Free space shown on two separate drives cannot be added together for this calculation.

For Sony’s storage compatibility table and move instructions, see Manage PS5 storage. Once the download starts, estimate the remaining transfer time.
