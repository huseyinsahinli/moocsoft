GTA 6 on PS5: what to do with a code-in-box copy

If your package says Code-In-Box, look for a PlayStation Store voucher. Sony says this GTA VI edition includes a download code and no disc. Have the account that will own the game ready before redeeming it.

Check three things before redemption

  1. Edition. Read the wording on your package or receipt. These steps cover the PS5 Code-In-Box edition.
  2. Region. Compare the voucher region with the region of your PlayStation account. A voucher must match the account region.
  3. Owner. On a shared PS5, check the signed-in account. The redemption is tied to that account.

Redeem and find the game

Open PlayStation Store on the PS5, open its menu and choose Redeem Code. Enter the 12-digit voucher and review what the confirmation screen offers. After redemption, open Game Library on that account and select GTA VI to download.

What does your screen show?

The code is missing or unreadable

Keep the packaging and proof of purchase. Contact the retailer about a missing insert. For a damaged voucher, follow PlayStation’s voucher support steps. Keep the full code private.

The voucher says it has already been used

Check the transaction history of the account used for redemption, then its library. On a family console, check which account was active at the time. If nobody in the household redeemed it, use voucher support with your receipt.

I can see the game, but it hasn’t started downloading

Select it in Game Library, then look in Downloads/Uploads. If the transfer is queued, inspect free storage and queue progress before attempting another redemption.
