GTA 6 character guide: Jason, Lucia and their connections

Start with two names: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Then follow two smaller circles, Jason’s connections in the Keys and the musicians around Only Raw Records. This guide uses Rockstar’s character introductions and leaves mission outcomes out.

Jason and Lucia

Jason has a military background and works in the Keys. Lucia wants a fresh start after leaving prison. When you read their profiles, notice what each expects from their partnership. Jason wants life to get easier. Lucia already has a plan.

Three connections that make the cast easier to remember

Jason ↔ Cal Hampton
Cal is Jason’s friend and also knows Brian. Hearing Cal’s name puts you in Jason’s local circle.
Jason ↔ Brian Heder
Brian runs a boatyard and gives Jason a place to live in return for work. That explains the link between Jason’s home and his obligations in the Keys.
Boobie Ike ↔ Dre’Quan Priest ↔ Real Dimez
Boobie and Dre’Quan are business partners. Dre’Quan signed Real Dimez. When Only Raw Records comes up, think of this music connection.

Can you place the names?

Who connects Jason’s housing to his work?

Brian Heder. Cal is the friend in this circle.

Is Real Dimez the name of the record label?

Real Dimez is the act. Only Raw Records is the label.
