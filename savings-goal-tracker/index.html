Using Savings Goal Tracker

Make the first step practical.

Connect the target to real contributions

Create a named goal, enter the amount you need and record money you actually set aside. Keep a planned contribution separate from a completed deposit so your progress reflects the balance available for the goal.

Review the plan as life changes

Use the contribution worksheet when a deadline changes or you miss a deposit. The 52-week guides offer increasing, reverse and flat examples you can compare with your budget. Savings Goal Tracker is an organizational record; it does not hold money or execute bank transfers.
