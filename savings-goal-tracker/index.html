No bank login required

Your money goals. Your own record.

Your goal records are stored locally on your device. Frugal tracks amounts you enter manually, not a live bank balance, and it does not hold or transfer funds. Check the current store privacy disclosures for advertising and usage-data information.

Questions

Turn a number into a plan.

Is Frugal free to download?

Yes. The free version contains ads and feature limits. Premium includes unlimited goals, an ad-free experience, detailed analysis, custom categories, goal images and PDF/Excel export. Check your store for current access and pricing.

Does Frugal connect to my bank?

No. Goal records are stored locally, and you enter contributions and withdrawals yourself. Recording an amount does not move money or confirm that a bank transfer happened.

Can I export my progress?

PDF and Excel exports are available with Premium. Export your records for a separate review or to share a report yourself.

Using Frugal

Make the first step practical.

Connect the target to real contributions

Create a named goal, enter the amount you need and record money you actually set aside. Keep a planned contribution separate from a completed deposit so your progress reflects the balance available for the goal.

Review the plan as life changes

Use the contribution worksheet when a deadline changes or you miss a deposit. The 52-week guides offer increasing, reverse and flat examples you can compare with your budget. Frugal is an organizational record; it does not hold money or execute bank transfers.
