About our guides

How Moocsoft Guides Are Prepared

Moocsoft publishes practical guides alongside its mobile apps and Flutter development service. We use AI assistance for writing and code. This page explains what readers can check, where examples come from and how to flag a mistake.

Who is speaking

The publisher is Moocsoft, Huseyin Sahinli’s independent app studio. Links to Moocsoft products promote our own apps or development service. These guides are not independent reviews of those products. About the studio.

AI assistance and examples

AI is used to help draft and revise explanations, generate code and create some editorial illustrations. Its output can be wrong. A label saying “AI-generated editorial illustration” identifies a scene created to illustrate a topic; it is not a product screenshot or evidence of a real event.

Worked amounts, sample meals, workout entries, puzzles and fictional stories are teaching examples. They are not customer records, personal experience or measured app results unless the page explicitly identifies a genuine source. Public app screenshots are labeled separately.

How to check an answer

Use the quantities, rules and assumptions printed next to a worked example. Arithmetic and website behavior have automated checks where supplied in the site’s source; passing a website check does not validate an app, a nutritional estimate or an individual outcome. You can inspect the public website source and checks.

References link to the source supporting the relevant explanation. A store listing supports a product feature or purchase boundary; it is not evidence for a health benefit or an independent product comparison. When a recipe, portion, schedule or rule is unknown, the answer must retain that uncertainty.

App information and free website tools

Store availability, pricing and features can change. Check the linked official listing for your platform before purchasing. A free website calculator or printable worksheet is separate from the mobile app. Inputs are not automatically imported into an app unless the page explicitly documents such a feature.

Limits and dates

Food, money, exercise and quitting guides cover general education and record keeping. They do not establish individualized medical, financial or training advice. A calculation can explain its inputs without establishing that those inputs fit your situation.

Publication dates identify the original guide. An updated date records a substantive revision to that guide. A design change, extra link or regenerated page does not by itself make the article newly researched. Neither a date nor this policy guarantees factual completeness or search performance.

Report something that does not add up

Contact Moocsoft with the page URL, the sentence or example in question and, when possible, a source or calculation showing the problem. No customer story, expert credential or review is implied by the Moocsoft byline.
