New Year Card Messages: 3 Notes for 2027

A good New Year note can be two sentences long. Name a moment from the year you shared, then offer one wish for the year ahead. If you can picture the recipient recognizing the memory, you already have enough to write.

Three New Year card messages to make your own
Who it’s forA message to adapt
A friendThose rainy Thursday walks got me through the autumn. Here’s to a few sunny ones in 2027.
A colleagueThanks for taking my first client call with me. I hope January brings you time for that trip you’ve been planning.
Someone far awayI made your lemon cake last weekend and thought of you. I hope we can share a slice in 2027.

Get the size right on a test sheet

Open the A4 file for A4 paper or the US Letter file for Letter paper. Select just the greeting-card page. Start with Actual Size or 100%, check the print preview, and run one plain-paper copy. Fold or trim it as shown and slip it into an envelope before committing your card stock.

Leave space for handwriting

Try your longest message on the plain-paper copy first. Leave room for your signature and a date. If several people are signing, have the first person write near the top. A child’s drawing or a last-minute line from a friend can then fit at the bottom.
