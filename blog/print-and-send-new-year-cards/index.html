How to Print and Send Your 2027 New Year Cards

A short New Year card is a good way to thank someone who made your year easier. Set aside one evening to print your cards and write a few lines while the details are still fresh.

Match the file to your paper

The New Year kit comes with A4 and US Letter PDFs. Pick the file that matches the paper in your printer and select the card page in the print dialog. Start with a single test sheet. Use Actual Size or 100% if you want to keep the designed dimensions, and check the preview for clipping before printing the batch.

Write one thing only you could say

Start with something specific the recipient did. For a friend who listened while you were changing jobs, try “I still think about our Thursday walks. Thanks for listening while I figured out my next step.” Add a wish for something they are looking forward to in 2027.

Set aside time to finish the cards

Trim or fold the test sheet as the design requires and check it against your envelope before printing the rest. If you plan to post the cards, write the addresses first and check your postal service’s holiday deadlines. Keep one or two extra cards for people you remember later.
