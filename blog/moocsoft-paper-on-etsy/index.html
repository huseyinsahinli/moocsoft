2027 Printable Calendars, New Year Cards and Goal Sheets

To send a January greeting, choose the New Year kit. To see all four weeks of February on dated planning pages, choose Perfect February. Both sets come in A4 and US Letter. The difference is what you want to have on your desk once the page comes out of the printer.

For a note that turns into a plan

The New Year kit pairs four greeting cards with a year calendar, a goals page and four cut-out goal cards. You might send a card to the friend who taught you to knit, then use a goal card of your own to schedule an evening to finish the scarf.

For a month you can see week by week

Perfect February works well for a project with several steps. Keep the monthly page on the fridge for shared dates and the current weekly page on your desk for tasks. The 28-day tracker gives you a separate place to record a recurring action.

What to check before you print

Both are paid digital downloads. Check the Etsy preview for the layout you want, choose the PDF matching the paper already in your printer, and select one page for a test. If you only need a weekly sheet today, enter its page number in the print dialog.

2027 New Year Printable Kit

Four greeting cards, a year-at-a-glance calendar, a goals page and four cut-out goal cards.

Set aside a card for someone you want to thank or catch up with. Keep the year calendar nearby, then use the goals page to write down a few priorities and a first step for each.

Perfect February 2027

A Monday-start calendar, four dated weekly pages, a 28-day habit tracker and a month-end review.

February 2027 starts on Monday the 1st and ends on Sunday the 28th. That gives the month four complete Monday-to-Sunday weeks. The weekly sheets follow those dates, and the tracker has room for three habits.
