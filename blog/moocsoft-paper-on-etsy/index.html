Introducing Moocsoft Paper on Etsy

Moocsoft Paper is our Etsy shop for printable cards, calendars and planners. The first two sets are designed for different moments: sending a New Year note, and putting the next four weeks on paper.

2027 New Year Printable Kit

Four greeting cards, a year-at-a-glance calendar, a goals page and four cut-out goal cards.

Set aside a card for someone you want to thank or catch up with. Keep the year calendar nearby, then use the goals page to write down a few priorities and a first step for each.

View on Etsy ↗

Perfect February 2027

A Monday-start calendar, four dated weekly pages, a 28-day habit tracker and a month-end review.

February 2027 starts on Monday the 1st and ends on Sunday the 28th. That gives the month four complete Monday-to-Sunday weeks. The weekly sheets follow those dates, and the tracker has room for three habits.

View on Etsy ↗

Both sets are paid digital downloads in English, with A4 and US Letter PDFs. You can preview the pages and see the current price on Etsy.
