Cookie Exchange: How Many Cookies Per Person?

For a Christmas cookie exchange with eight bakers, ask each person to bring 21 cookies if everyone takes home three from each of the seven other bakers. Add eight more per baker if you want everyone to taste one of every kind at the party.

Calculate the batch before sending invitations

Multiply the number of other bakers by the cookies you want from each batch. For six bakers taking two of each other’s cookies, each baker brings 5 × 2 = 10. Count a couple baking one recipe together as one baker. If someone cancels after baking day, divide the spare portions at the table.

An invitation you can copy

“Cookie swap at my place on December 12 at 3 p.m.! We have eight bakers. Bring 21 cookies of one kind, packed in seven groups of three, and an empty container for the trip home. Tell me what you’re baking by December 5 so I can make the table labels.” Add tasting quantities if you plan to serve samples.

Set up a table guests can navigate

Put the cookie name and the baker’s recipe card beside each batch. Place tasting samples on a separate plate so nobody eats a portion meant for the swap. Invite guests with ingredient questions to speak to the baker directly. When everyone is ready, walk around the table once, picking up one packet from each of the other bakers.
