How to Host a Christmas Cookie Exchange

A cookie exchange gives everyone a chance to share a recipe and take home a mixed box. Agree on the number of bakers and the size of each take-home batch so guests can decide how much to make.

Work out how many cookies to bring

Ask bakers to write their names and planned cookies on the sign-up sheet. If six bakers each bring two cookies for each of the five other bakers, that is ten per person for swapping. Ask for a few extra only if you want a separate tasting plate. Tell guests what size container to bring.

Put a recipe card and label beside each plate

Give each baker a recipe card and a fold-over label. Write the cookie name on both so guests can match a favorite to its recipe. Share ingredient information directly when guests request it, and flag shared equipment or unknown ingredients if food allergies come up.

Divide the cookies before taking them home

Set out six empty containers or labeled places on the table, then give each baker a turn to add their cookies. If the group wants to vote for a favorite, use the voting slips during the tasting. Use the take-home tags to keep the finished boxes easy to identify.
