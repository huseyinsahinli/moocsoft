How to Organize Secret Santa with Printable Wish Lists

A short wish list gives a Secret Santa enough direction to shop confidently. Before sending the forms, tell participants when the exchange is happening, whether participation is optional and how much to spend.

Give people a few ways to answer

Choose the office or family questionnaire, or print the two half-page forms if you have a larger group. Ask people to name a hobby, a favorite snack or a type of gift they would use. Someone who likes puzzles might ask for a small crossword book and add that they already own a daily puzzle calendar.

Collect forms before drawing names

Set a deadline and draw from the people who have opted in. Use the match organizer as the private record so you can check that each person gives and receives one gift. Send the giver only the wish list for the person they drew.

Make the exchange easy on the day

Tell participants where and when to leave wrapped gifts. Write the recipient’s name clearly on the gift tag. For an office exchange, have the organizer arrange an alternate drop-off time for anyone who will be away. Decide ahead of time whether the group wants to reveal the givers after opening.
