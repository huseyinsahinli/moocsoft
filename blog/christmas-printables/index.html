5 Printable Christmas Activities for Your Holiday Gathering

If your guests arrive at four and dinner starts at six, what happens in between? A mystery gives early arrivals something to solve together. A cookie swap works better when everyone has agreed to bake ahead. Start with the part of the gathering you want help with, then choose a set below.

For guests who arrive at different times

Set a small table for The Missing Tree Star. Put the briefing, clues and a pencil there, and keep the answer key with the host. If people arrive halfway through, one player can bring them up to speed using the deduction grid.

For a gathering built around food

Choose the cookie exchange kit when guests are bringing the treats. Send the sign-up before anyone shops. For a sit-down dinner, start with the dinner planner instead. Write the serving time first, then work backward to the dishes that share the oven.

For the gift exchange or Christmas Eve

Use the Secret Santa forms before you draw names so each giver gets a few useful clues. For a quieter evening at home, put one activity ticket in a Christmas Eve box with a handwritten note. Let the person opening it choose when the activity starts.

Christmas Cookie Exchange Kit

A baker sign-up sheet, recipe cards, fold-over labels, voting slips, take-home tags and an award.

Send the sign-up sheet before the swap. At the party, put a recipe card and a fold-over label next to each plate before guests vote.

Christmas Eve Box Kit

Two box labels, six tags, eight activity tickets, a family note, a checklist and a memory page.

Wrap a box you already have, write a short note and tuck one of the tickets inside. The story ticket gives someone the job of picking the bedtime book.

Christmas Dinner Planner

A menu planner, categorized shopping list, oven schedule, serving timeline, guest list and place cards.

Write down your serving time, then work backward on the oven schedule. Use the timeline to give each helper a task before everyone sits down.

Secret Santa Questionnaire

Separate office and family wish lists, two half-page forms, a match organizer and six gift tags.

Hand out the office questionnaire before drawing names. For a larger group, print the two-up quick form and keep the match sheet with the organizer.

The Missing Tree Star

A Christmas detective game with six witness clues, two logic grids, a letter code and a full answer key.

Hand each team the witness notes, worksheet and code. Keep the hints and answer key aside until the teams are ready to check their theories.
